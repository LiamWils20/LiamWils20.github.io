Time Reaper is a story-driven realm, where you the player must scale and traverse lands of La-jor, for revenge with the added bonus of saving the human race along the way.


This game is being developed as part of a masters course with SAE Institute, want to Learn More?

Controls

  • WASD - Movement
  • Space - Jump
  • Left Mouse - Attack
  • Mouse - Camera Control
  • Shift - Sprint
  • 1 thru 5 - Abilities
