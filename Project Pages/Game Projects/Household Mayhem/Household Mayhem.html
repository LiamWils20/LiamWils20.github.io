About

In a world, where no-thing can be trusted. Not even your favourite fork. Use homemade weapons and defences to fight back against hordes of enemies and survive the crazy whacky new world.

Project Backstory

Household Mayhem, was a collaborative project created for the Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2

The results for "Household Mayhem", are shown in the image below;



With a theme of "Trust No One", we decided to take the theme and convert it slightly into a "Trust No Thing" theme, where the player is in a world where nothing can be trusted, not even their favourite fork. The player must use homemade weapons and defences to fight back against hordes of enemies and survive the crazy whacky new world.
